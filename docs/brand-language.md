## 2. Brand Voice

### Voice in one line
Confident operator talking peer-to-peer to another media buyer — direct, specific, zero fluff, quietly expert.

### The 5 voice pillars

| Pillar | Means | Do | Don't |
|--------|-------|-----|-------|
| **Advertiser-to-advertiser** | We run ads too; we speak the buyer's language | "You can tell these people actually run ads themselves." | Corporate/agency-speak, "solutions provider synergy" |
| **Specific > vague** | Real numbers, real timeframes, named products | "Delivered in 1–4 hours", "BM1 to BM2500", "6+ months, zero disables" | "Fast delivery", "high quality", "the best" |
| **Pain-aware** | Name the exact thing that hurts advertisers | "Skip the disabled-account loop", "refuse to babysit disabled accounts" | Generic "grow your business" |
| **Calm confidence** | Reassure through proof, not hype | "The stability is unreal.", "GOADS infrastructure just holds." | Exclamation-heavy, "REVOLUTIONARY!!!" |
| **Honest & fair** | State limits plainly (replacement-only, no refunds) | "Refunds are not provided under any circumstances." | Overpromising, hidden asterisks |

### Tone knobs
- **Formality:** low-mid. Contractions welcome ("you're", "can't", "we've").
- **Energy:** steady, not shouty. One exclamation max per section.
- **Person:** "you"/"your" for the reader, "we"/"our"/"the GOADS team" for us.
- **Sentence length:** short, punchy. Fragments allowed for rhythm ("No cost, no catch." / "Clean history, ready to run.").
- **Comma-splice fragments** are a signature move: *"Aged, ID-verified profiles to pair with your BM. Clean history, ready to run."*

### Signature rhetorical moves
1. **Triplet value stacks:** "Stable assets, real support, instant replacement." / "No tickets, no waiting."
2. **Pain → relief pivot:** name the loop, then remove it. *"BM banned? Ad account disabled? We recover the asset so you keep the pixel."*
3. **Rhetorical question openers** (FAQ + cards): "BM banned? Ad account disabled?" / "What is a verified Business Manager?"
4. **Us-vs-the-alternative:** the villain is always *Telegram sellers / random suppliers*, never a named competitor.
5. **Proof in numbers:** "$50M+ ad spend powered", "500+ advertisers", "2,000+ users", "5+ years".

### Banned words / phrases
- ❌ "cheap", "guaranteed" (we say *warranty / replacement*), "hack", "loophole"
- ❌ "revolutionary", "game-changing" as our OWN claim (customers may say it in reviews)
- ❌ overusing "!" — max one per block
- ❌ vague filler: "world-class", "cutting-edge", "seamlessly leverage", "robust solutions"
- ❌ refund promises — policy is **replacement-only**

## 4. Value Propositions (the 4 pillars)

Every page reinforces some subset of these. Lead with the one most relevant to the page.

1. **Stability** — "Accounts never go down." Verified BMs survive 10× longer; run 6+ months with zero disables.
2. **Speed** — Delivery in 1–4 hours; instant replacements; onboarding tools that save ~5 hrs/day.
3. **Real support** — 24/7, sub-2-hour response, from people who actually run ads. Telegram / WhatsApp / Discord — "no tickets, no waiting."
4. **Full-stack coverage** — Meta + Google + TikTok assets, recovery, verification, and free tools from a single provider.

- `500+` advertisers who trust GOADS
- `2,000+` extension users · `5/5` stars

## 7. Proof / Social Proof Voice (review style)

Reviews are the tonal north star for how customers should *sound* — short, concrete, results-driven. Names span Europe / USA / LATAM; channels are Telegram or WhatsApp. Patterns to echo:

- **Switching narrative:** "Switched to GOADS after losing accounts to Telegram sellers. It's infinitely better…"
- **Hard numbers:** "Ran three BM5s for six months straight with zero disables." / "scaled past $40k/day on a single account with no warnings."
- **Support at odd hours:** "Support answered me at 2am and fixed everything."
- **Time saved:** "recovering banned assets… used to add roughly 5 hours to my workload every single day."
- **Signature closer line:** "GOADS infrastructure just holds." / "Worth every penny!"

Testimonial attribution format: `Name` — `Role, Company` (e.g. *Lukas Hoffmann — Agency Owner, Hoffmann Digital*).

## 8. FAQ / Policy Voice

Plain, reassuring, honest about limits. Structure: direct answer first, then the mechanics/conditions.

**Signature policy lines (keep wording tight):**
- Warranty: *"If your account gets disabled within 7 days of delivery for reasons unrelated to policy violations on your end, we will replace it for free — no questions asked."*
- Honesty on limits: *"Our warranty covers replacement only — refunds are not provided under any circumstances."*
- Responsibility handoff: *"Once an asset has been delivered and begins to be used, the responsibility for its operation… belongs to the client."*
- Delivery: *"Most accounts are delivered within 1–4 hours after payment confirmation."*
- Differentiator: *"We combine 5+ years of experience, a 7-day warranty policy, sub-2-hour support response times, and a full-stack platform covering Meta, Google, and TikTok — all from a single provider."*

FAQ question phrasing: real buyer questions, conversational — "Which BM tier should I buy?", "What happens if my BM gets disabled?", "Do you offer bulk discounts?"
